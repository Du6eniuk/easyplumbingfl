Brand exploration · Round 1

Easy Plumbing FL — logo concepts

Three directions, each with a palette that carries straight into the website. Every mark is vector, holds up at favicon size, and works on light and dark backgrounds.

01

Fresh Drop

Friendly · modern · clean

The lowercase e of “easy” is drawn as a single water drop. Rounded lowercase type keeps it approachable: plumbing that feels simple, not intimidating. Sunshine yellow is kept for buttons only, so “Call now” always stands out.

02

Sunshine Coast

Local · warm · coastal

A Florida sunset set inside a water drop: water for the trade, sun and surf for the state. The retro script-and-caps pairing feels like a local, family-run shop. Teal and sunset orange stand apart from the sea of blue-and-red plumbers.

03

Hot & Cold

Bold · established · trade

An E built from real pipe runs and fittings, with red and blue caps borrowed from hot and cold water lines. Condensed capitals read from across the street on a van or yard sign. The most “serious contractor” of the three.
